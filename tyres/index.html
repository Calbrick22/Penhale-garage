Law on Tyres

In the UK, tyres are permitted to wear down to a minimum of 1.6mm across the central three quarters all the way around the circumference. However, we recommend never letting your tread fall below 3mm to maximise safety. If your tread falls below the minimum legal requirement then you could face a fine of up to £2,500 per tyre! In addition, you could also be issued with three penalty points on your licence.
