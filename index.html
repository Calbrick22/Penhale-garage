If you are looking for a friendly, local garage in Fraddon, Cornwall, then visit Penhale Garage for expert services at competitive prices. Established since 2013, we pride our business on providing affordable vehicle servicing, repairs and MOTs. Fully equipped to handle everything from cambelt and clutch replacements to exhaust repairs, brake replacements, diagnostics, tyres and welding, you can trust your car is in safe hands. To book your vehicle in for servicing or repairs, get in touch with the team.

Our garage is easily accessible and located just off the A30 near Kingsley Village which has a selection of shops and restaurants you can visit while we work on your vehicle.
