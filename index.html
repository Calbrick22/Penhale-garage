Warning light on?

Tap a light to see what it usually means. Our diagnostic check pinpoints the exact fault for £25 + VAT.

Book a check About diagnostics

Engine management light

The engine's computer has recorded a fault. It can be something minor, such as a sensor, or something that needs attention soon.

If it flashes, reduce speed and get the car checked straight away.

Oil pressure warning

Oil pressure is too low. Driving on can cause serious engine damage.

Pull over safely and switch the engine off as soon as you can.

Battery / charging warning

The charging system isn't working properly. This is often the alternator or its drive belt.

The car may only run for a short time on battery power, so get it checked soon.

Brake system warning

This can mean the handbrake is on, the brake fluid is low, or there is a fault in the braking system.

If it stays on with the handbrake off, don't drive until it's been checked.

Tyre pressure warning

One or more tyres may be under-inflated.

Check and adjust your tyre pressures. If the light comes back, you may have a slow puncture.

ABS warning

The anti-lock braking system has a fault. Your normal brakes should still work, but ABS won't help in an emergency stop.

Drive carefully and book a check soon.

Common meanings only. Lights vary between cars, so check your handbook.
