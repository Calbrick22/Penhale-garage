Car Servicing

Visit Penhale Garage for expert car servicing at competitive prices. We service all makes and models of vehicles and use the very best techniques and vehicle diagnostics to achieve total client satisfaction. As well as offering mini, interim and full servicing, we can also perform winter and summer checks to ensure your car is safe to drive and functioning as it should all year round. Car Servicing Packages

As well as being able to follow your vehicle manufacturer’s servicing schedule to retain any remaining warranty, we also offer three levels of servicing for all makes and models. With car servicing starting from just £120, you won’t need to look anywhere else!

Mini Service

A simple service if you need your car checked out before a long journey or between interim and full servicing.

  • 35-point check
  • Oil change
  • Oil filter replacement
  • Air filter change

Interim Service

Recommended annually or every 10,000 miles, whichever comes first.

  • 39-point check
  • Oil change
  • Oil filter replacement
  • Air filter change
  • Spark plugs replaced

Full Service

Recommended every two years or 20,000 miles, whichever comes first.

  • 43-point check
  • Oil change
  • Oil filter replacement
  • Air filter change
  • Spark plugs replaced
  • Cabin filter replaced
  • Windscreen wash top-up
  • Fuel filter

Servicing Extras

Offering flexible servicing packages, we can also provide:

  • Cambelt replacement
  • Cambelt replacement & water pump
  • Complete brake fluid replacement
  • Coolant replacement

Why Do I Need Servicing?

Regular vehicle servicing is essential to keep your vehicle working as it should, it will also help the vehicle retain its value. In addition, a service can identify issues that can be addressed before they pose a problem. So, for trouble-free motoring book your vehicle in with Penhale Garage today.
