Car Garage St Stephen

Welcome to Penhale Garage. Our local car garage is within easy access from St Stephen, Cornwall, and provides professional garage services for all makes and models of vehicles. Whether you need vehicle repairs or servicing, we offer expert services at competitive prices. To book an appointment or request a free quote, please get in touch.

About Us

We have a fully equipped garage that provides all aspects of vehicle care and maintenance. We are RAC approved, and also members of the RMI and Trust My Garage Scheme. In addition, we are a VOSA approved MOT Centre, so visit us for services you can rely on.

Our Garage Services

We offer a wide range of garage services for all makes and models of vehicles, old and new. Our services include but are not limited to the following:

  • MOTs
  • Vehicle servicing
  • Vehicle repairs
  • Cambelts
  • Brakes
  • Clutches
  • Exhausts
  • Steering
  • Engine repair
  • Suspension
  • Welding
  • Diagnostics
  • Tyre fitting and inspections
  • Laser wheel alignment
  • Air Conditioning
  • Local breakdown recovery

Get a Quote for Car Servicing & Repairs

If you would like to find out how much our services cost, then get in touch to request a no obligation quote. We can provide estimates over the phone or if you bring your vehicle in to the garage, we can assess the job and provide a fixed price quotation.

Book a Service

If your car needs attention, simply pick up the phone to book a no obligation quotation. We can book your vehicle in for servicing or repairs at a convenient time for you.
